# Phase 14 Plan 01

## Objective

Ship the first coherent CRM operations surface and reduce obvious abuse paths while full auth is still pending.

## Deliverables

- `/crm` page with pipeline board, queues, connector health, detail panel, and timeline
- `PATCH /api/crm/accounts/[id]` for workspace edits
- Same-origin validation on key browser-facing mutating routes
- In-memory rate limiting on pitch generation and CRM mutations/sync routes
