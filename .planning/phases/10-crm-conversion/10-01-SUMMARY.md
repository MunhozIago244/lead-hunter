# Phase 10 Plan 01 Summary

**One-liner:** Added explicit lead-to-CRM conversion with a route, deduplication, default primary-contact creation, and a dashboard CTA with immediate CRM feedback.

## What Was Built

- `lib/crm.ts` to validate payloads and convert `leads` into `crm_accounts`
- `POST /api/crm/accounts/from-lead` as the CRM entry point
- Deduplication by `lead_id` first and `company_name + city` second
- Automatic creation of a default primary contact if one does not exist yet
- `LeadCrmBox` in the lead detail panel with action button, result messaging, and CRM summary cards
