# Phase 10 Plan 01

## Objective

Create the first operational CRM entry point so a user can take a qualified lead and promote it into a managed CRM account.

## Deliverables

- `POST /api/crm/accounts/from-lead`
- server-side conversion helper with deduplication and primary-contact creation
- dashboard CTA: `Adicionar ao CRM`
- immediate CRM summary feedback in the lead detail panel
